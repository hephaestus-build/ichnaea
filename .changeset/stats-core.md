First statistics core. `compareCounts` compares one binary measure per estimand. For `fixed-cases`, every completed trial counts: each case with at least one completed trial per arm enters the stratified Miettinen–Nurminen score interval and test with equal case weight (the mean of per-case differences), and a case with none in an arm is excluded and reported. The regression test is the exact stratified conditional test, or a within-case permutation test when trial counts differ. For `sampled-cases`, Tango's skewness-corrected interval from 300 cases (experimental); below 300 cases or 30 families the comparison is estimate-only. Each result names its regression method, says whether it lies inside the validated domain, and lists why not. Proportion, score and ordinal measures have no validated method yet. Also Wilson intervals (descriptive), Holm and Benjamini–Yekutieli adjustment, closed-form floors, a simulated minimum detectable effect and a seeded Mersenne Twister. Checked against ratesci and R's exact Mantel–Haenszel test, by exact enumeration, and by simulation under a two-stage rule (see VALIDATION.md).
