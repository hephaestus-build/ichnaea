First statistics core: the stratified Miettinen–Nurminen score interval and test for the `fixed-cases` estimand, the exact stratified conditional regression test, Wilson intervals, Tango's paired score interval and sign-flip randomisation for `sampled-cases` (both marked as not yet validated), Holm and Benjamini–Yekutieli adjustment, zero-noise floors and a simulated minimum detectable effect, and a seeded Mersenne Twister. Checked against 186 reference intervals from R's ratesci and by simulation studies (see VALIDATION.md).
