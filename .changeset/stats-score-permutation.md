`scorePermutationTest` is the within-case permutation test for measures with one value in [0, 1] per trial: scores, proportions reduced to one value per trial, and equally spaced ratings. It is exact on a lattice of values when that is cheap, and seeded Monte Carlo otherwise. Its type-I study has not run yet, so nothing decides with it: score, proportion and ordinal measures stay estimate-only.
