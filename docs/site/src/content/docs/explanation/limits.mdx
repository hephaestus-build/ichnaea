A limit is the largest decrease in a measure that you accept.
For example, a limit of 15 points means: a candidate may answer up to 15 points worse than the base and still pass.

The limit is a decision about your product, not about statistics.
The owner of the measure sets it.
Statisticians call it a non-inferiority margin, and the suite file and the records store it as `margin`.

## Register it before you compare

You write the limit in the suite, with a reason, before any comparison uses it.
The support example registers its limit like this:

```json
{
  "measure": "support/facts-stated",
  "margin": 0.15,
  "marginStatus": "registered",
  "marginReason": "Twelve cases cannot see smaller drops; the floor at three replicates is about 0.08."
}
```

A limit that you choose after you see the results can always be made to pass.
That is why a limit with the status `placeholder` gives no verdict. Ichnaea runs the plan as an estimate only.

## The floor: the smallest limit your suite can clear

Even when nothing changes and every trial passes, your suite cannot clear every limit.
With K cases and r trials per case, the smallest limit that can pass is about

> floor = z² / (K(r − ½) + z²), with z = 1.645

| Cases | Floor with 2 trials | Floor with 3 trials |
| ----- | ------------------- | ------------------- |
| 17    | 9.6 points          | 6.0 points          |
| 20    | 8.3 points          | 5.1 points          |
| 60    | 2.9 points          | 1.8 points          |
| 250   | 0.7 points          | 0.4 points          |

If your limit is below the floor, your suite can never pass, even when nothing changes.
The planner checks this before the first trial and says so.

## Why no candidate can pass yet

A non-inferiority result is validated only for limits from a smallest validated limit up to 30 points.
That smallest limit is not set yet.
The studies found case structures in which the lower bound is too optimistic.
A statistical pass requires randomized blocks and a validated limit.
The current test can detect a drop only within these runs.

## How to read the bound

Ichnaea computes a one-sided 95% lower bound for the change.
If the bound crosses minus your limit, the trials leave open a larger drop.
A test that detects a drop reports `DROP_IN_THESE_RUNS_ONLY` with an Inconclusive verdict.
The suite can stop CI on this result with `onRunDrop: "block"`.

## What it does not cover

- A limit of 0 is not a non-inferiority test.
- A claim that a candidate is better than the base is never validated.
